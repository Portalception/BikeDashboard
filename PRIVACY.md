# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Bike Dashboard is a fuel and servicing tracker for motorbikes. This policy explains what happens to your
data when you use it.

## The short version

**Bike Dashboard doesn't collect, send or share any of your data.** Everything you enter stays on your
phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't ask for internet
access.

## What the app stores

Everything is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

- Fill-ups (date, country, litres, amounts paid, odometer, fuel left, petrol brand)
- Service records, service intervals and categories
- Bike details you choose to enter (such as name, plate, registration and paperwork dates), including
  details read from a log card PDF you pick
- Your settings and appearance choices

None of this is sent to the developer or anyone else.

## Files you choose to import or export

You can import a spreadsheet, a backup or a log card PDF, and export backups as JSON or CSV files. The
app only reads or writes the files you pick, using Android's file picker. Where an exported file goes
(for example your phone storage, Google Drive or a messaging app) is up to you, and is covered by that
service's own privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to remind you when a service or paperwork renewal is due.
- **Run at startup:** to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.

## Android backup

If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your Google
backup, as it does for other apps. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, and all of its data is deleted from your
phone. Any backup files you exported yourself stay wherever you saved them.

## Children

The app isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
