## Full description (max 4000)

Bike Dashboard is a simple, private tracker for your motorbike's fuel and servicing, made for riders
who fill up in Singapore and Malaysia.

Logging a fill-up takes seconds: one question per card, in the order you do it at the pump.

FUEL
• Singapore: litres, full tank or partial, optional petrol brand, then the price, with an optional
  amount after your card or membership discount, so you can see how much you saved.
• Malaysia: ringgit paid, your bank's exchange rate (it remembers the last one) and what the pump
  charged, converted to SGD automatically.
• Partial fills are handled properly: economy is worked out at your next full tank.
• Gentle warnings when something looks off, like more litres than your tank holds.

DASHBOARD
• Fuel economy, cost per km, cost per day and km per day, for the last fill, week, month or lifetime.
• Petrol savings from filling up in Malaysia, minus the cost of the trip.
• Charts for economy, price per litre, spending and more. Arrange or hide cards and charts.
• A full history of fill-ups, with edit and delete.

SERVICING AND PAPERWORK
• Log services, oil changes and repairs.
• Set service intervals by km or time and get reminded when they're due.
• Keep bike details and get reminders before road tax or insurance runs out.

MADE YOURS
• More than one bike, including rentals, each with its own numbers and look.
• Light, dark and pure black themes, your own colours and alternative app icons.
• Animated riding backgrounds, like a racetrack or rain on your visor.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking. The app doesn't even ask for internet access.
• Everything stays on your phone. Back up to a file whenever you like, and restore it on a new phone.

Bike Dashboard is open source: https://github.com/Portalception/BikeDashboard
